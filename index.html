02 / Behind the work

The person
behind
the frame.

I have always been interested in storytelling my entire life.

Throughout my life I have experimented with many forms of creative expression, but there was one that finally stood out as the solution. I landed on screenwriting and film.

I had always liked making and editing random little videos, and finally realized that it was the perfect medium of storytelling for me.

They can immerse you into a new world, with characters you have never seen before and may now explore their lives.

SKILLS & PRODUCTION EXPERIENCE

Adobe Premiere Pro / Adobe Photoshop / Camera Operation / Lighting / Microphone Operation / Digital Editing

Media Producer / Director for the Cedarville Supermileage Team, filming race footage and editing promotional videos. Previously a Content Producer intern at Brain Trust, producing marketing and social media videos, employee vision videos, and a podcast sizzle reel. Additional camera and technical production experience through volunteer work at Crossroads Church and River’s Crossing Community Church.
